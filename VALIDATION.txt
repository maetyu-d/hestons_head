Fractional distillation
-----------------------
* Three appended machine kinds preserve the saved identifiers of every previous
  machine: fractionating still, adjustable reflux column and fraction receiver.
* A still routes its selected heads/hearts/tails volatile stream through the
  clockwise side port while its main outlet retains the stillage. Each compound
  and aroma mass is partitioned between the two physical batches.
* Reflux columns process vapour only. Their stage count changes the compound
  partition between enriched vapour and water-rich liquid reflux; the latter
  exits the clockwise port and can return to its still.
* Cooling condensers condense that same vapour batch to liquid. Fraction labels
  survive condensation, and receivers hold, serve or forward the actual batch.
* Vapour cannot enter a tasting dock or another still directly; incompatible
  routing applies backpressure until food reaches a condenser. A liquid feed to
  a reflux column is likewise held instead of being misprocessed.
* Plans includes a 26-machine train with one feed, three cuts, three reflux
  returns, three condensers and four separate collection vessels (three cuts
  plus final stillage). It can be edited and stamped like any floor plan.
* Cut, stage, temperature, vapour and fraction metadata are serialised with real
  process history. Old saved foods default to liquid with no fraction label.
